LocalShelf Support

LocalShelf plays your own MP4 videos and MP3 audio on iPhone with iOS 27 or later. On your Home Screen, it appears as Local Video Player.

Import files and folders

Open the media library, tap the add button, and choose files or a folder from Files. You can also copy files into the app’s folder in Files or transfer them using Finder on a Mac. Open LocalShelf to process the transfer.

Use subtitles

Open an MP4, show the playback controls, and open the tracks menu. Choose a subtitle or add an SRT or WebVTT file. You can adjust external subtitle timing there.

Get help

Email walter2743@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS and app versions, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Please leave out passwords and private media.

LocalShelf 使用帮助

LocalShelf 可在搭载 iOS 27 或更新版本的 iPhone 上播放本地 MP4 视频和 MP3 音频。主屏幕名称为 Local Video Player。

打开媒体库，轻点添加按钮，从「文件」选择文件或文件夹。也可把媒体复制到「文件」中的应用目录，或通过 Mac 的 Finder 传输，再打开应用完成导入。

播放 MP4 时，打开轨道菜单可选择字幕、添加 SRT 或 WebVTT 字幕，并调整外挂字幕的时间。

请将 iPhone 型号、系统和应用版本及复现步骤发至 walter2743@icloud.com。请勿发送密码或私密媒体。
