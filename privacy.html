LocalShelf Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy covers LocalShelf 1.0 for iPhone and this support website.

Information on your device

LocalShelf stores imported media and subtitles, the library index, playback progress and preferences on your device. It does not require an account, send these items to the developer, or include ads, tracking, or developer-operated analytics.

If you set an unlock method, the app stores credential information in the iOS Keychain. When you use Face ID, iOS performs the biometric check and shares the authentication result with the app. The app receives no face scans or biometric templates.

File providers and deletion

You choose where to import and export files. iCloud Drive and other providers you select in Files handle those files according to their own policies. You can delete library copies in the app. Original files, exports and system backups are managed separately.

Support and this website

If you email support, the developer receives your address and the information you provide and uses it to respond and troubleshoot. This site has no forms or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages may process standard request information to serve and protect the site.

For privacy questions, email walter2743@icloud.com.

LocalShelf 隐私政策

2026 年 11 月 14 日生效。适用于 iPhone 上的 LocalShelf 1.0 及本支持网站。

导入的媒体和字幕、媒体库索引、播放进度及偏好保存在设备上。应用无需账号，不向开发者发送这些信息，也没有广告、追踪或开发者运营的分析。

设置解锁方式后，应用使用 iOS 钥匙串保存凭据信息。面容 ID 由 iOS 验证，应用仅取得认证结果，不读取面部扫描或生物识别模板。

你可选择导入和导出位置。通过「文件」选择 iCloud 云盘或其他提供方时，对方按其政策处理文件。可在应用中删除媒体库副本，原文件、导出副本和系统备份分别管理。

发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮件地址及所提供的信息，用于回复和排查问题。本网站没有表单和广告脚本，GitHub Pages 可能为提供和保护服务而处理常规请求信息。

隐私问题请联系 walter2743@icloud.com。
